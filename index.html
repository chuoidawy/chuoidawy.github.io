About

A little background.

I'm Son Nguyen, a third-year electrical engineering student at UBC Okanagan. Originally from Vietnam, I moved to Canada for high school in Toronto.

My work centers on electronics hardware: avionics with the UBCO Rocketry team, soft robotics research funded by the IUSRA 2026 award, and an early-stage start-up where I'm the Electrical Lead. Most of what I build ends up as a PCB — designed in Altium, built to fly, move, or sense.
